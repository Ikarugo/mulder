## Analysis of Misses and Errors

### Where the budget went

The 315 calls (323 in the audit log) went to `search` (58), `open_case` (24), `get_raw_output` (23), `get_investigation_summary` (15) and 15 `submit_finding`s (10 delivered; 3 `update_finding`s, no deletions), against run 1's 222 with 73 searches and run 3's 300 with 60. The rm2 extraction agent queued `run_registry_parser`, `query_registry_value`, `run_mft_parser`, `run_prefetch_parser`, `run_amcache_parser` and `run_shimcache_parser` against the USB stick ("SYSTEM hive not found via TSK extraction or mount ... suggesting this may be a non-Windows image"), so no ShimCache or Amcache was ever produced for the PC; the PC agent's `USBSTOR`, `TimeZoneInformation`, `InstallDate` and `ShutdownTime` queries all returned and none of the four values is in the report. `tsk.fsstat` was not run (run 1's source for the label); `parse_pst` was not called; `optical.listing` was read and reported. The report's evidence sits on `tsk.filelist`, `tsk.masquerade`, `optical.listing`, `ez.mft`, the disc's `bulk.email`/`bulk.rfc822` and `bulk.duplicates`; the PC-side registry, EVTX, search-history and email carves are indexed and untouched.

### File dates read as events (FP 1; items 5, 6, 8, 10, 13, 15)

Run 3 turned the disc's file dates into a January "Initial Discovery" phase. This run does the same at larger scale: the document mtimes on the disc and RM2 become "file creation" on January 5, "deletion waves" on January 20 and 23, the binaries' MFT dates become "Eraser installed January 12" and "Google Drive installed February 19", and the five are threaded into a cause-and-effect narrative ("installed Eraser to securely delete evidence from the USB device") that the counter-analysis then confirmed by re-reading the same dates. Every March 22–25 event the key has, except the March 24 copy to RM2 and the March 24 burn, is either moved to January or February (Eraser, Drive, the deletion) or absent (the OS install, CCleaner, the search history, the connections). The `InstallDate` value that would have collapsed the timeline was retrieved at 23:11 and not opened.

### Govdocs content as the headline (FP 2; items 16, 19)

The disc's `bulk.email` (12 lines) and `bulk.rfc822` (41 lines) were the rm3 agent's first reads, and their OMB, Library of Congress and `whitehouse.gov` strings became the run's one CRITICAL finding, both Email IOCs and a containment action to notify two federal agencies. The disc listing that the same agent read correctly (directories, sessions, files) is subordinated to it: the documents are "government communications and archival materials" rather than the Secret Project set, so item 16 stays PARTIAL, and the PC's own `bulk.rfc822`, where the correspondent sits, was never opened (item 19).

### Seen in-run, not reported (items 2, 8, 12, 18, 20)

Five facts reached the run log and not the report: `InstallDate` (item 2), CCleaner (item 8, "CCleaner installed (2015-03-13)" in two summaries), `USBSTOR` (item 12), the share (`\\10.11.11.128\secured_drive\Secret Project Data\` at 23:14, item 18) and `TimeZoneInformation` (item 20). The share and CCleaner were in the cross-system agent's hands with the timeline it was building and were not submitted; the three registry values were fetched by the extraction batch and never queried by name afterwards.

### RM1 back, RM2 named, and neither identified (items 3, 4, 12, 15)

Run 1 lost RM1 entirely; this run has both sticks by label on the right images, RM2 with its FAT32 partition extent, and the 17 disguised files on RM2 with all 17 types. What no MiniMax run has yet done in the same report is tie a label to a serial and a connection time: run 3 had the serials without the labels or images, this run has the labels and images without the serials, and both had `USBSTOR` on hand. Item 15 has the right device and window again and still no deletion event: the deletion is dated to January and credited to Eraser.

### Registry basics (items 2, 20)

Five `query_registry_value` calls, four of them the right keys on the right hive, and nothing from any of them in the report. "NIST domain credentials" and "Active Directory" are asserted with no query and against the WORKGROUP the key states; the FAT times are labelled UTC with the zone retrieved and unread.
